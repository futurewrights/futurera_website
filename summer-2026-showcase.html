Students came with ideas.

In Summer 2026, our inaugural Build with AI — From Idea to Launched App bootcamp brought together 10 students ages 13–16 for an intensive, hands-on introduction to building with AI.

Over two weeks, students learned to turn ideas into plans, work with AI as a development partner, debug and improve their work, and bring their own projects to life.

What students learned

The apps are the evidence.
The capabilities are the outcome.

Building software gave students something tangible to create. But the broader goal was to develop the ways of thinking required to work effectively with AI.

01

AI Literacy

Understand what generative AI can do, where it can fail, and why human judgment remains essential.

02

Prompting & Context

Communicate goals clearly and provide AI with the information and constraints it needs.

03

Planning

Translate an idea into requirements, steps, and an actionable build plan.

04

Decomposition

Break a larger problem into smaller components that can be understood, built, and improved.

05

Debugging & Iteration

Test results, diagnose problems, revise approaches, and learn from unsuccessful attempts.

06

Communication & Ownership

Explain what was built, justify decisions, demonstrate the work, and identify opportunities for improvement.

Showcase FAQ
Are these projects still live?

Yes — each app was deployed to a real URL during the bootcamp, and most students keep them running (and keep improving them) after camp ends. If a link above doesn't load, the student may have taken it down or moved on to a new project — let us know and we'll update it.

Did students really build these without coding experience?

Yes. Every project here was built by a student who started the bootcamp with no prior coding experience, using GitHub Copilot inside Visual Studio Code to turn plain-English descriptions into working apps.

How were these projects built?

Over two weeks, students learned to prompt AI tools effectively, plan a build, break it into pieces, and debug it — then spent the second week designing, building, and launching their own capstone app. See the day-by-day curriculum for the full breakdown.

Can my child build something like this?

That's exactly what the bootcamp is for. Check out the next cohort for dates, pricing, and how to apply.
