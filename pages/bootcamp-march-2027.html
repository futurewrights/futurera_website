THE FUTURE WRIGHTS EXPERIENCE

Build with AI. Learn to make the decisions.

In-person guidance, purposeful projects and opportunities for students to show the thinking behind what they create.

SMALL COHORTS

Guidance throughout the build.

With up to 15 students per group, learners can ask questions, discuss ideas and get help moving forward. Instructors demonstrate the process and guide students as they plan, build, test and improve their apps.

EDUCATORS + PRACTITIONERS

Learn how to build—and how to think through it.

University-affiliated faculty help shape the curriculum, while educators and industry practitioners connect learning to practical AI workflows. Students practise choosing priorities, evaluating AI suggestions and understanding how their app works.

CREATE · TEST · EXPLAIN

A working app. A story of how it was built.

Students develop their own app, test its features and improve it through feedback. In their final demonstration, they show what works, explain their decisions and describe how AI helped. Online sharing follows agreed consent and privacy arrangements.

WHAT STUDENTS CREATE

An idea they choose. An app they can show.

Students begin with a mini-app to learn the process, then develop their own small browser app with instructor guidance. They choose its purpose, plan essential features, test the results and improve it.

Possible projects include:

A game to play

Create a memory challenge with rules, a score and a restart button. Decide how it should work, then test the player’s experience.

A tool for learning

Build a quiz or flashcards around a topic they enjoy. Choose the content, design the interaction and check the feedback.

A story to explore

Design an interactive adventure where choices lead to different outcomes. Plan the paths and test what happens at each decision.

Each project gives students something to demonstrate—and decisions, tests and improvements they can explain.

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN

Learn to direct AI—and understand what you build.

Through their own project, students practise turning ideas into clear instructions, making thoughtful decisions and checking whether the results work.

01

Define the idea

Decide who the app is for, what it should do and which features belong in the first version.

02

Guide the AI

Write clear instructions, review AI’s proposed plan and decide which suggestions to accept, change or question.

03

Understand the app

Explain how a user’s actions connect to the app’s rules, what it remembers and what appears on screen.

04

Build step by step

Break the project into manageable parts, check each addition and save working versions along the way.

05

Test and improve

Compare what should happen with what actually happens. Investigate problems, test repairs and use peer feedback to improve the experience.

06

Launch and explain

Prepare the app for sharing, demonstrate its features and explain key decisions, AI contributions and remaining limitations.

QUESTIONS PARENTS ASK

Everything you need to know.

Does my child need coding experience?

No. The program is designed for students ages 13–16 with no prior coding experience. Instructors help students get started, communicate their ideas to AI and understand the essential behaviour of their app.

What will my child build?

Students begin with a mini-app, then develop an individual browser app with instructor guidance. Possible projects include a game, learning tool or interactive story. Instructors help keep the project achievable within the week.

How do you check that students understand what they build?

Students explain their goals, review AI suggestions and test the results. During short Show · Explain · Change checkpoints, they demonstrate a feature, explain their thinking and respond to a small new requirement. Instructors use these conversations to guide feedback and support.

Does my child need to bring a laptop?

No. Future Wrights provides a MacBook for use during the program, along with the required course software, Codex and OpenAI API access.

Is this a full-day program?

Students attend one three-hour session each day, Monday–Friday, March 15–19, 2027. Choose either the morning group, 9:00 AM–12:00 PM, or the afternoon group, 1:00–4:00 PM.

Will my child’s app be available online?

Students work toward publishing a small web app with instructor guidance. Online sharing follows agreed consent and privacy arrangements. Where publication is approved, students receive a live link others can use to try their app.

What does the tuition include?

The $999 CAD tuition, including applicable taxes, covers the five-day bootcamp, use of a MacBook, required course software and access, a certificate of completion and a personalised learning report.

How do we apply?

Select Apply Now to submit an application. Each group has up to 15 students, with applications accepted on a rolling basis until full.

If you would like to discuss the program first, book a 20-minute information session or email admissions@futurewrights.ai.

What is your refund and cancellation policy?

All cancellations and refunds are subject to the following policy:

  • Cancellation requests: All cancellations must be submitted in writing by email to admissions@futurewrights.ai.
  • At least 30 days before the cohort starts: A refund will be issued, less a $100 CAD administrative fee.
  • Fewer than 30 days before the cohort starts: No refunds will be provided.
  • Missed days: Pro-rated refunds or credits are not available for days absent.
  • Cancellation by Future Wrights Academy: We reserve the right to cancel a cohort if minimum enrolment is not met or exceptional or unforeseen circumstances arise. If we cancel, we will accommodate the student in an alternate cohort or provide a full refund.
