What makes this cohort different

A small, in-person cohort, not a self-paced video.

Three things distinguish Future Wrights from the AI courses parents see advertised online.

Small Cohorts

Faculty + Practitioners

Bootcamp FAQ
Is a coding background required?

No prior coding required. You build by describing what you want in plain English, and GitHub Copilot — the same AI coding assistant used by professional developers worldwide — helps turn it into real, working code. The skills students practice (clear prompting, planning a build, breaking it into components, and finding and fixing bugs) are exactly what working builders do.

What do students need to bring?

A laptop — Mac or Windows, any modern model. We use Visual Studio Code as our editor, and while it can technically run in a browser on Chromebooks, the desktop version gives students the reliable, professional-grade workflow the bootcamp is built around. Chromebooks aren't ideal — please bring a Mac or Windows laptop. We confirm device details with each applicant on the application form, so if you're unsure whether your laptop will work, just tell us and we'll let you know. If a laptop is a barrier, mention it when you get in touch — we can usually arrange a loaner for the bootcamp duration.

What tools are used?

Students use GitHub Copilot — the same AI coding assistant used by professional developers worldwide — running inside Visual Studio Code. Alongside it, we introduce ChatGPT for planning and brainstorming. All AI tools we use are approved for students 13+ under their terms of service, and each student's account is set up with parental consent captured on the application. Every tool is included in tuition — there's no software fee on top. After camp, students can keep building with GitHub's free Student Developer Pack, so the skills travel home.

Where does it run? Can students from across the GTA attend?

Yes — students from across the GTA are welcome. Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Oakville, and beyond. We pick venues that are transit-accessible (subway and GO Transit), with a real classroom space — not a hotel ballroom.

The venue is Webtree Academy, at 60 Scarsdale Rd, Unit 100, North York, ON, M3B 2R7 — a real classroom space in North York, transit-accessible by subway and bus.

What if a student misses a day?

We provide a recap and a 1:1 catch-up session with a mentor. The bootcamp is intensive — we strongly encourage full attendance, but life happens, and we don't penalize for one missed day. Missing more than two days is usually a sign the timing isn't right; we'd rather defer enrollment than rush.

Is parent attendance required or invited?

Not required. Parents are warmly invited to Demo Day on the final day — students give a short pitch and parents try the app each student built, live, in front of the room. The day is loud. There are stakes. It's the moment that makes the whole bootcamp make sense.

What's the application process?

A short written application (a few sentences about why the applicant wants to take it and what they'd hope to build), then a short conversation with our program lead. Decisions within two business days. Rolling intake until the cohort fills.

What does it cost?

Tuition for the March 2027 cohort is $899 CAD. This covers the full five-day bootcamp with all materials and tools included. Payment is collected by Interac e-Transfer after we accept your application; we'll send the recipient details with your admission. Need-based scholarships are available; mention this on your application or email us directly.

Refunds & cancellations

All cancellations and refunds are subject to the following policies:

  • All cancellations must be made in writing, via email, to Future Wrights Academy at admissions@futurewrights.ai.
  • Refunds, less an administrative fee ($100 CAD), will be issued for all cancellations made at least 30 days before your cohort's start date.
  • After the 30-day window, no refunds will be provided.
  • Pro-rated refunds or credits are not available for days absent.
  • Future Wrights Academy reserves the right to cancel a cohort if the minimum enrollment is not met or in the event of exceptional and/or unforeseen circumstances. If we have to cancel a cohort, we will accommodate the student in an alternate cohort, or provide a full refund.
Do students receive a certificate of completion and skills report?

Yes. Students receive a Future Wrights certificate of completion and a personalized skills report highlighting the skills and capabilities they developed throughout the week.

What happens after the bootcamp?

The apps each student builds keep running on their public URLs after the bootcamp ends — friends can use them, siblings can use them, students can keep iterating. Many keep building on their capstone or start new projects with the same tools through the school year.
