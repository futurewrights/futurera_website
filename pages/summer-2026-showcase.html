The learning journey

Learning AI by building with it.

Students moved from an idea to a working project — learning to direct AI, evaluate its output, solve problems, iterate, and take ownership of what they created.

Understand what AI can do, where it can fail, and how to communicate with it effectively.

02 BUILD

Turn ideas into working software through planning, experimentation, debugging, and iteration.

03 PRESENT

Demonstrate the finished project, explain key decisions, reflect on challenges, and share what comes next.

What students learned

The apps are the evidence.
The capabilities are the outcome.

Building software gave students something tangible to create. But the broader goal was to develop the ways of thinking required to work effectively with AI.

01

AI Literacy

Understand what generative AI can do, where it can fail, and why human judgment remains essential.

02

Prompting & Context

Communicate goals clearly and provide AI with the information and constraints it needs.

03

Planning

Translate an idea into requirements, steps, and an actionable build plan.

04

Decomposition

Break a larger problem into smaller components that can be understood, built, and improved.

05

Debugging & Iteration

Test results, diagnose problems, revise approaches, and learn from unsuccessful attempts.

06

Communication & Ownership

Explain what was built, justify decisions, demonstrate the work, and identify opportunities for improvement.

Program design

Two weeks. A deliberate progression.

See the full day-by-day curriculum on the bootcamp page.

Week 1 — Learn to Build with AI
  1. 1OrientationMeet the cohort, set up, and get ready to build
  2. 2What Is AI & How Does It Think?Develop a personal webpage
  3. 3Taking It LiveSet up GitHub and Vercel, then deploy a personal webpage
  4. 4The Art of PromptingPractice giving AI clear, useful instructions
  5. 5Plan Before You BuildTurn an idea into a clear plan before writing code
Week 2 — Build, Debug & Launch
  1. 6Teach AI How You WorkGive AI the context it needs to support your process
  2. 7Think in Pieces, Build in PiecesBreak a larger build into manageable parts
  3. 8Everything BreaksDebug, iterate, and learn from what goes wrong
  4. 9Capstone ProjectBring an original idea to life
  5. 10Demo DayPresent the work you built and what you learned
