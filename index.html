Parent FAQs

Questions parents ask

Who are your programs for?

Our programs are designed for students in Grades 8–12, with specific age ranges and requirements listed for each program. They welcome young people interested in creating, exploring ideas and learning how to work with AI. Your child does not need to be planning a career in technology.

Does my child need coding or technical experience?

Our introductory Build with AI course requires no prior coding experience. Instructors help students get started and explain unfamiliar concepts as they work. Requirements may differ across programs, so check the relevant program page before applying.

What will my child do in a program?

Students take part in guided projects and practical challenges, with time to explore ideas, make decisions and use feedback. Activities depend on the program. In Build with AI, for example, students develop a small web app, test its features and demonstrate their work.

How do you help students use AI thoughtfully?

Students practise setting goals, giving AI clear instructions and checking what it produces. Instructors help them question suggestions, recognise limitations and decide what to accept or change. Students remain responsible for their choices and for explaining their work, while AI supports exploration and creation.

How will you know what my child understands?

Instructors ask students to explain their goals, decisions and results throughout the learning experience. During project work, students may demonstrate a feature, describe how it works or explain how they checked an AI-generated result. These conversations guide feedback and further support. Each program page explains the learning records included.

Who designs and teaches your programs?

Our programs are designed and delivered by university educators and industry mentors. Team members contribute teaching, research and practical technology experience, with roles varying by program. Our team page introduces the people involved and distinguishes program leadership, teaching and advisory roles.

How do you handle privacy and consent?

Students are guided to avoid sharing personal information with AI tools and to use fictional sample data in projects. Before publishing student work, we explain what will be publicly visible and request parent or guardian consent. Permission to use names, photographs, videos or testimonials for promotion is requested separately.

Where can I find fees and financial-support information?

Each program page lists its tuition and what is included. Please contact us to ask whether discounts or financial support are available for a particular cohort. Program-specific payment, cancellation and refund terms are provided with the enrolment information.

How do I choose a program and apply?

Explore the available programs and review their age range, activities, schedule and fees. When you find a suitable option, follow its application link. If you would like help deciding, book a 20-minute parent information session to discuss your child’s interests, experience and what to expect.
